Our Story

Founded in 2015, Interactive Systems emerged from a simple observation: traditional interfaces were limiting human potential in digital environments. Our founders, a team of engineers and designers passionate about human-computer interaction, set out to revolutionize how people engage with technology.

What started as a small research project has evolved into a leading company specializing in touch, multi-touch, and multi-process solutions for large format displays. Our breakthrough came when we developed proprietary algorithms that could accurately detect and process multiple simultaneous touch points on screens up to 100 inches in size.

Today, Interactive Systems serves clients across education, healthcare, retail, corporate, and entertainment sectors. Our solutions power interactive kiosks, digital signage, collaborative workspaces, and immersive experiences around the world.

We continue to push the boundaries of what's possible, investing heavily in research and development to bring you the most advanced touch technologies available.
